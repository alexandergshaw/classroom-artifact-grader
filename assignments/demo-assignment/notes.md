# Demo Assignment – Instructor Notes

## Overview

This assignment asks students to write a short essay (500+ words) on relational
database design concepts, covering normalization theory and key relationships.

## Automated checks (70 pts)

| Check                    | Points | Criteria                                           |
|--------------------------|--------|----------------------------------------------------|
| `min_word_count`         | 15     | At least 500 words in the submission               |
| `has_introduction_heading` | 15   | Document contains a heading titled "Introduction"  |
| `has_reflection_heading` | 15     | Document contains a heading titled "Reflection"    |
| `required_database_terms` | 20    | Uses: database, primary key, foreign key, normalization |
| `has_citations`          | 5      | At least 2 inline citations (APA or bracket style) |

## Manual review (30 pts)

Instructors award these points based on:

- Accuracy and depth of the technical discussion (15 pts)
- Quality of writing, organization, and clarity (10 pts)
- Originality and critical reflection (5 pts)

## Expected outputs

The `expected/` directory is reserved for future reference solutions.
Place a model answer here when one becomes available.

## Scoring notes

- The automated score is always shown as *earned / 70* (the manual 30 pts are
  never automatically awarded).
- A total rubric score of 100 is only achievable after an instructor completes
  the manual review step.
